Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

MindVault is built around intellectual exploration, personal reflection and independent thought. Privacy is an important part of that foundation. This policy explains what information may be collected, why it may be processed, how it is protected and what choices users have.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to MindVault, including its educational content, interactive exercises, user accounts, discussions, anonymous submissions and Private Diary features.

MindVault is intended for users aged 13 and above. Some features may have additional age restrictions.

2. Information we may collect

The information processed depends on which features a user chooses to access.

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers and account-related settings.
  • Profile information: optional display name and preferences.
  • Private Diary information: notebook titles, diary entries, timestamps and associated files when users choose to upload them.
  • Community information: debate replies, philosophy submissions, anonymous reflections and reports.
  • Experiment information: answers and results generated when users voluntarily participate in interactive experiments.
  • Technical information: limited operational information needed to maintain security, prevent abuse and operate the service.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, government identification numbers, financial information or unnecessary sensitive personal details in public submissions.

3. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Save and synchronize Private Diary entries.
  • Display educational content and experiment results.
  • Operate debates and community submissions.
  • Review reports and moderate inappropriate content.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Detect abuse, protect infrastructure and maintain security.
  • Process account deletion requests.

Information should only be used for purposes reasonably connected to operating and protecting MindVault, unless a separate lawful basis or user consent applies.

4. Private Diary

Private Diary is intended to be a personal writing space. Entries are not intended to be publicly displayed or included in community discussions.

  • Diary entries are associated with their account owner.
  • Other ordinary users should not be able to access them.
  • They are not intended to appear in public search results.
  • They are not intended to be published in the Anonymous Diary.
  • They are not intended to be used for advertising profiles.
  • They are not intended to be used to train AI models.

MindVault does not intend to analyze private diary content for personality profiling or targeted recommendations.

Important technical limitation: Privacy controls must be implemented and tested on the server and database, not merely hidden in the website interface. MindVault must not claim end-to-end encryption unless that feature has been independently implemented and verified.

5. Anonymous submissions

Certain community features may allow a user to submit content without displaying their account identity publicly.

Public anonymity does not necessarily mean that the platform cannot associate a submission with its original account for moderation, abuse prevention or security purposes.

Anonymous submissions may be reviewed before publication. Content that violates platform rules may be rejected, restricted or removed.

6. Email and notifications

Email may be used for account authentication, essential security notices, support responses and optional notifications.

If experiment results are offered by email, the user must be given a clear choice to receive them. Private Diary content must never be included in experiment-result emails.

Optional communications should not be enabled without the required consent.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Essential technologies may be used to maintain authentication, security and basic website functionality.

Optional analytics or advertising cookies should not be introduced without appropriate disclosure and consent where required.

MindVault's intended approach is to avoid unnecessary tracking, behavioural advertising and invasive profiling.

Read the Cookie Policy for additional information.

8. Third-party services

MindVault may rely on carefully selected service providers for infrastructure, authentication, database hosting, email delivery and file storage.

Such providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Their own privacy and security practices also apply.

Third-party services should be disclosed accurately before production deployment. No provider should be described as having security certifications or protections that have not been verified.

9. Data sharing

MindVault does not intend to sell personal information or share it with advertisers for behavioural advertising.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary for:

  • Operating the platform through service providers.
  • Responding to valid legal requirements.
  • Protecting users, the platform or public safety.
  • Investigating serious abuse or security incidents.

Any such sharing should be limited to the information reasonably required for the relevant purpose.

10. Data retention

Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for its stated purpose, applicable legal requirements and legitimate security needs.

Account deletion is intended to include a 15-day recovery period after a verified request. During that period, the account may be restricted according to the deletion process.

After the recovery period, account information and associated Private Diary data are intended to be permanently deleted, subject to documented legal or security retention requirements.

Public contributions may be removed or handled according to the applicable retention and anonymization rules.

The deletion schedule must be implemented and verified before MindVault promises that automated deletion is operational.

11. Security

MindVault intends to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Encrypted connections using HTTPS.
  • Secure authentication and session management.
  • Database access restrictions and row-level security.
  • Restricted access to administrative functions.
  • Input validation and protection against common web attacks.
  • Appropriate backups and incident-response procedures.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use a unique password and protect access to their email account.

12. Children's privacy

MindVault is intended for users aged 13 and above. Certain features may have additional restrictions based on age or applicable law.

MindVault should not request unnecessary identity documents from minors. Age-related safeguards should use proportionate methods and collect as little information as reasonably possible.

If a parent or guardian believes that information has been collected improperly from a child, they may contact the support address below.

13. User choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other handling of their personal information.

Users may also be able to:

  • Update available account details.
  • Delete or edit their own diary entries.
  • Withdraw optional communication consent.
  • Submit reports about inappropriate content.
  • Request account deletion.

14. International processing

Depending on the infrastructure providers selected, information may be processed in countries other than the user's country of residence.

MindVault should disclose relevant hosting locations and applicable safeguards once its production infrastructure has been selected.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MindVault introduces new features, changes its infrastructure or needs to reflect legal requirements.

Significant changes should be communicated through an appropriate platform notice or other suitable method.
