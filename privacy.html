Privacy Policy

MindVault believes that intellectual exploration should not require surrendering personal privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you access MindVault, why it is processed, how it is protected and what choices are available to you.

Effective date: To be completed before launch.
Last updated: To be completed before launch.

Our privacy principles

MindVault does not intend to sell personal information, use private diary entries for advertising, build behavioural advertising profiles or use personal submissions to train artificial intelligence systems.

01. About MindVault

MindVault is an independent educational and intellectual exploration platform focused on philosophy, psychology, neuroscience, perception, discussion and personal reflection.

Website: https://mindvaultspace.com/
Privacy contact: supportmindvaultspace@gmail.com

The legal identity and jurisdiction of the platform operator must be published here before the service launches.

02. Information We Collect

MindVault aims to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate its features, maintain security and provide requested services.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Account information: Your email address, authentication records and account identifier.
  • Authentication information: Verification codes, code expiry information, session information and security records.
  • Private Diary information: Notebook titles, entries, uploaded backgrounds, timestamps and saved changes.
  • Community information: Debate contributions, replies, submissions, moderation records and notification preferences.
  • Anonymous submissions: Content submitted through anonymous features. Anonymous public display does not necessarily mean that no internal account or security records exist.
  • Experiment information: Responses and results when you voluntarily use an interactive exercise.
  • Technical information: Limited device, browser, connection and security information where required to maintain the service.

03. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

MindVault does not require users to publish their real names, phone numbers, photographs, public birth dates or demographic profiles to participate in ordinary educational content.

Do not submit passwords, financial information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through public discussion or submission features.

04. How We Use Information

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Sending and verifying email authentication codes.
  • Saving and synchronising private diary entries.
  • Displaying discussions and user submissions.
  • Delivering requested notifications.
  • Providing experiment results where applicable.
  • Preventing spam, fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Investigating reports and enforcing community rules.
  • Responding to support and privacy requests.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

05. Private Diary Protection

The Private Diary is intended to be accessible only to its authenticated owner.

Private diary entries are not intended to appear in public feeds, community discussions, anonymous submissions, search engine results or other users' accounts.

Access controls must be enforced by the server for every diary operation, including viewing, editing, uploading, exporting and deleting content.

Private Diary content is not intended to be used for advertising, behavioural profiling or artificial intelligence training.

Cloud storage and synchronisation may involve authorised infrastructure providers. MindVault must disclose its actual storage, encryption and access arrangements before launch.

Unless explicitly implemented and independently verified, MindVault does not claim that diary entries are protected by end-to-end encryption or that technical administrators are incapable of accessing stored data.

06. Anonymous Features

Certain areas may allow users to submit content anonymously or under a chosen display identity.

Anonymous display means that the user's identity is not intentionally displayed alongside the public contribution.

For moderation, account security and abuse prevention, limited internal records may still associate a submission with an account or technical information.

Users should avoid including identifying details in content they choose to publish anonymously.

07. Psychology Experiments and Perception Exercises

MindVault provides educational exercises involving perception, cognitive processes, personality-related concepts and reflection.

These exercises are educational and are not intended to diagnose medical or psychological conditions.

Responses should only be processed for the functionality requested by the user. Any optional saving or email delivery of results must be clearly explained and require appropriate consent.

MindVault does not intend to analyse private diary entries to generate psychological profiles.

08. Artificial Intelligence

MindVault is not intended to operate as an AI therapist, diagnostic system, automated psychologist or AI debate participant.

Personal diary entries, private reflections and account information are not intended to be used to train AI models.

If third-party AI services are introduced in the future, their purpose, data access and applicable privacy terms must be disclosed before any relevant information is shared.

09. Cookies and Similar Technologies

MindVault may use essential technologies required for authentication, session management, security and basic website functionality.

Optional analytics, advertising trackers and non-essential cookies should not be activated without the appropriate notice and consent where required by law.

Additional information is available in our Cookie Policy.

10. Third-Party Service Providers

Operating MindVault may require infrastructure and service providers for hosting, database storage, authentication, transactional email, file storage and security.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their contracted services and under applicable agreements and legal requirements.

The actual providers, their purposes and relevant data processing arrangements must be documented before launch.

11. Data Sharing

MindVault does not intend to sell or rent personal information.

Information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary:

  • To service providers operating essential platform functions.
  • To investigate security incidents and abuse.
  • To comply with valid legal obligations.
  • To protect users, the platform or other individuals.
  • Where the user has expressly requested or authorised sharing.

12. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and security obligations.

Retention periods for authentication records, moderation records, backups, diary content and account information must be documented in the platform's operational policies.

13. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their accounts through the available account settings or by contacting the support team.

MindVault intends to provide a 15-day account deletion recovery period after a verified deletion request.

During this period, the account may be restricted or deactivated according to the deletion procedure. A verified cancellation request may stop the deletion process.

After the recovery period, the account and associated personal data are intended to be permanently deleted from active systems, subject to applicable legal, security and backup-retention requirements.

Public contributions may require separate treatment where deletion, anonymisation or retention is necessary to preserve discussion integrity or comply with legal obligations.

The actual deletion procedure must be implemented and tested across databases, stored files, backups and connected services.

14. Security

MindVault intends to apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, which may include:

  • HTTPS for data transmission.
  • Secure authentication and session management.
  • Access restrictions for private user content.
  • Server-side validation and authorisation.
  • Protection against unauthorised access and abuse.
  • Appropriate database and file-storage safeguards.
  • Security monitoring and backup procedures.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Security claims must reflect the controls actually deployed and tested.

15. Children and Teen Users

MindVault is intended to provide educational content for users aged 13 and above, subject to applicable local laws and parental-consent requirements.

The platform should collect only the information necessary to operate its services and should not publicly display users' ages or dates of birth.

Any future age-restricted area must have effective age eligibility safeguards and must not be accessible to underage users.

16. International Data Processing

MindVault may be accessed by users in different countries. Depending on hosting and service-provider arrangements, information may be processed or stored in countries different from the user's country of residence.

Applicable safeguards and transfer requirements must be followed where required by relevant data protection laws.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of eligible information.
  • Raise a privacy complaint with the relevant authority.

To make a privacy request, contact: supportmindvaultspace@gmail.com

18. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when platform features, security practices, service providers or legal requirements change.

Significant changes should be communicated through an appropriate website notice or other suitable method.
