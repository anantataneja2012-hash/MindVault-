Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used on MindVault.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how MindVault may use cookies and similar technologies when visitors access the website.

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. They can help websites maintain sessions, remember preferences and protect accounts.

2. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be required for the website to function safely and correctly.

Depending on the services implemented, they may support:

  • Secure account authentication and session management.
  • Protection against unauthorised access.
  • Security checks and abuse prevention.
  • Remembering essential privacy or accessibility settings.

These cookies are not intended for advertising or behavioural profiling. Disabling them may affect login and other essential features.

3. Optional Cookies and Analytics

MindVault's intended approach is to avoid unnecessary tracking. Optional analytics, advertising or personalisation cookies should not be enabled without the appropriate user choice and consent.

If optional services are introduced, this policy should identify the service, the information it collects, its purpose and how visitors can change their preferences.

4. Third-Party Services

Some website functions may rely on external providers, such as authentication, hosting, email delivery or secure file storage. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their services.

The actual providers and their relevant privacy policies must be identified before MindVault launches. Their use should be limited to what is necessary for the selected service.

5. Managing Cookies

Visitors can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings. Browser controls differ, so consult the documentation for the browser being used.

Where optional cookies are implemented, MindVault should provide a clear way to accept, reject or change optional cookie choices. Rejecting optional cookies should not prevent access to public educational content.

6. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

Websites may use local storage, session storage or similar browser technologies. These are not technically identical to cookies.

MindVault should document any such technology, explain its purpose and avoid storing private diary entries, authentication secrets or other sensitive information in insecure browser storage.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website functionality, providers or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
