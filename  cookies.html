Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

MindVault aims to keep its technical footprint minimal. This policy explains cookies and similar technologies that may be used to provide essential functionality, maintain security and improve reliability.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in a user's browser. They can help websites remember information between visits or maintain an active session.

Similar technologies include browser storage, session identifiers and other mechanisms used to maintain website functionality.

2. Essential cookies

Essential cookies and similar technologies may be required for basic platform operations.

Examples include:

  • Maintaining an authenticated session.
  • Protecting account access.
  • Supporting security and fraud prevention.
  • Maintaining essential website preferences.
  • Supporting request integrity and session validation.

These technologies should only be used when reasonably necessary for the requested service or functionality.

3. Optional cookies and technologies

Optional technologies may include analytics, personalization or other non-essential functionality.

MindVault's intended approach is to avoid unnecessary tracking and behavioural advertising.

Optional technologies should not be activated without appropriate user choice or consent where required by applicable law.

4. Authentication and sessions

If a user signs in, MindVault's authentication provider may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain the session.

Secure session configuration should use appropriate protections, including:

  • HTTPS-only transmission.
  • Secure cookie settings where supported.
  • HttpOnly protection for session cookies where applicable.
  • Appropriate SameSite settings.
  • Session expiration and revocation controls.

Exact cookie names, providers and expiration periods depend on the authentication infrastructure actually deployed.

5. Third-party services

MindVault may use third-party providers for hosting, authentication, database infrastructure, email delivery and other essential services.

Some providers may use their own cookies or technical identifiers when their services are accessed.

The final production cookie inventory should identify relevant providers, purposes and durations.

6. Cookies and Private Diary

Private Diary functionality may require authentication and session technologies to ensure that diary operations are associated with the correct account.

Cookies should not be used to expose diary content to other users or to create behavioural advertising profiles.

7. Managing cookies

Users can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings.

Browser controls commonly allow users to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies from selected websites.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Configure automatic deletion.

Blocking essential cookies may prevent account authentication or other platform features from functioning correctly.

8. Data protection and tracking

MindVault does not intend to sell personal information or use invasive behavioural advertising.

Cookie-related information should only be processed for disclosed purposes and according to applicable privacy requirements.

Implementation note: A written policy does not disable tracking technologies. The actual website, hosting services, external scripts and browser requests must be audited to establish which technologies are active.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MindVault introduces new technologies, changes service providers or needs to reflect legal requirements.

Significant changes should be communicated through an appropriate platform notice.
