The silent observer

04 · PHILOSOPHY BATTLES

Two positions.
More than one lens.

The point is not to defeat an opposing idea. It is to understand what makes it persuasive.

POSITION A

Free Will

People exercise meaningful agency and can be held responsible for choices, even within a world shaped by causes.

VS
POSITION B

Determinism

Events, including human decisions, arise from prior conditions and causal relationships.

Consider the strongest version of each position before forming a conclusion.
